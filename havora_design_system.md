## 🌍 Brand Positioning: Africans Home & Abroad

**HAVORA** is designed as a world-class intentional relationship platform built specifically for single Africans across the continent (Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, South Africa, etc.) and throughout the global diaspora (USA, UK, Canada, Europe).

### Core Brand Values
1. **Dignity & Intentionality**: Moving away from casual swiping apps toward intentional courtship and marriage.
2. **Cultural Resonance**: Celebrating African heritage, shared family values, and cross-border connectivity.
3. **High Security & Trust**: Mandatory biometric & government ID verification to eliminate catfish and fake profiles.
